About the game

Block Theft City is an open-world sandbox city. It plays on a computer (keyboard and mouse) and on a phone or tablet (touch). Every feature works on both; the touch version is shown in brackets.

On foot
  • W A S D walk, Shift run, Space jump (it also climbs onto cars and ledges). Mouse looks, the wheel zooms. (Drag the left half to move, the right half to look, pinch to zoom; Run and Jump buttons.)
  • F is the use key: get in and out of vehicles, steal a parked car, call a lift, board the metro, open the ATM and every shop. (Use button.)
  • C sits on benches, train seats and the floor. E eats food. O makes a pedestrian your friend (up to 3). (Sit / Stand button and the ⋯ menu.)
  • V first or third person, B front view, Y drone view that flies anywhere and teleports you. Hunger drains faster the more you run.
Driving
  • W S accelerate and brake or reverse, A D steer, Space handbrake and drift, Shift nitro, H horn, L lights, R radio (Neon FM). (Steer buttons, Gas, Brake / reverse, Nitro, Horn.)
  • Cars, vans, trucks, bikes and a city bus. Each has its own tank: refuel at a petrol pump, press X for a mechanic, or drive into a garage. G starts a bus route when you drive a bus. K (cheat) spawns vehicles.
Weapons
  • Pistol, revolver, shotgun, SMG, assault rifle, sniper rifle, machine gun, flamethrower and rocket launcher. 1-9 pick one, Q / T cycle, J or left click fires, right click aims, P scopes (2x-30x), N buys 30 pistol rounds. (Fire, Aim, Scope buttons and the ⋯ weapons list.)
Money, jobs and shops
  • 1000 jobs of ten kinds (delivery, courier, taxi, race, car theft, getaway, speed trap, bike messenger, collect, bounty), bus routes and coins around the city earn neon.
  • The street market has the Neon Bank (ATM, loans, transaction history), Gun Shop, Ammo Shop, food stalls and stalls you can buy for a payout every 5 minutes. One stall is a clothes shop with nine outfits; your signature look is unique to you.
  • The vehicle showroom sells cars (floor 1) and bikes (floor 2) and can be bought for 60,000 neon; its garage repairs your vehicle automatically.
Health, police and the city
  • Hospitals (red cross on the minimap) have a medical store selling medicine. You can fall ill, and if your health runs out you wake up at a hospital minus 10% of your neon. Ambulances carry injured people there.
  • Police react to crimes, up to five stars and a helicopter. Busted means five years in prison and half your neon. Police barricade gates close some roads.
  • Metro, glass lifts, an overbridge, parking lots, petrol stations, day and night, weather (clear, rain, storm, snow) and seasons.
Menus and saving
  • Esc pause (the pause button), Tab Settings, F1 How to play, I cheat menu, M mute, U full screen, F8 screenshot, F9 record. On a phone all of these are in the ⋯ menu and the pause menu.
  • Neon, jobs, weapons, stalls, loan and outfits save automatically. Save game keeps your position in 5 slots; Autosave saves every 30 seconds.
  • Settings has Graphics (quality, shadows, draw distance, frame rate, dynamic resolution and lowest sharpness, fog, sharpness, brightness, field of view), Audio, Controls (look options and key bindings), World (season, traffic, crowds, Real life simulation), Interface (coordinates, autosave, FPS counter, HUD, minimap, eyes) and Capture. How to play explains every feature in detail, and on a phone ⋯ > Touch guide shows the touch controls.
